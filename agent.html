Autonomous paper agent — 30-day pre-registered experiment

A Claude decision layer receives mechanical scanner signals on SPY during regular hours and decides take/skip/manage with written reasoning for every decision, executing on an Alpaca PAPER account (no real money anywhere). "Robot" controls take every signal blindly — they exist so the agent's skips can be scored: a skipped signal's value is whatever the robot ate taking it.
