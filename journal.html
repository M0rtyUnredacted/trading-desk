THE CHANGE I MADE ON FRIDAY

Up until Friday I sized every trade the same way: one contract, maybe add a second if it was working. That means a Nasdaq loss and a Dow win are different sizes of money even if they're the same quality of trade.

Friday morning I switched to sizing by risk. One percent of the account per trade, about $99 right now. The platform does the math. If the stop I want costs more than that on one contract, I don't get to take the trade. Three trades a day, then I'm done.

I want to be honest about what that does and doesn't do. I ran the numbers on my own trades before switching. Equalizing the dollars doesn't make the expected profit go up. What it does is make my results match my decisions instead of matching which instrument the wins happened to land on, and it keeps the variance down. That's the reason to do it.
