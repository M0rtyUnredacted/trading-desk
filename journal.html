2026-08-21-correction

Correction to this thread.

I said entering off the 1-hour was me bending my own rule — that I'm supposed to enter on the 5 or the 15.

That was wrong, and here's why it was wrong.

The order was a resting limit at 7,696.50. That price doesn't move when I change what timeframe the chart is on. It fills where it fills. The entry, the stop, the target, the management — none of it would have been any different if I'd been staring at a 5-minute chart the whole time.

What actually happened is that my indicator wouldn't draw the 1-hour structure block on the lower timeframes, so I couldn't SEE a level I had already priced. That's a display annoyance. It's not a discipline problem, and calling it one was me inventing a mistake.

The distinction I'd missed: "which timeframe do I enter on" is a question about discretionary entries, where the timing of the click is the decision. A resting limit at a higher-timeframe level doesn't have an entry timeframe at all. It just has a price.

Grade for the day goes A− → A. The minus was there for that one reason and no other.
